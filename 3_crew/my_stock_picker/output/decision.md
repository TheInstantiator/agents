Palantir Technologies: 80 - Strong position in AI-powered data analytics with significant government and commercial traction, especially in the U.S. Its unique technology and accelerating commercial growth provide a compelling narrative. However, the commercial viability of quantum computing is still years away, and its current valuation (P/S ~100-120x) is extremely high and speculative, suitable only for highly aggressive, long-term investors.

D-Wave Quantum Inc.: 55 - A first-mover in quantum annealing with strong recent revenue growth and a "Strong Buy" consensus from analysts. However, its market cap is disproportionately large compared to its current revenue, indicating extreme speculation. It's a high-risk, high-reward play with persistent losses, suitable only for very aggressive, long-term speculative portfolios.

Rigetti Computing, Inc.: 45 - A quantum computing pioneer with significant technological ambitions but currently declining revenues and an extremely high price-to-sales ratio (>9,000x), indicating severe overvaluation. While it has a cash reserve, its unprofitability and the early, uncertain stage of the quantum market make it a highly speculative and risky investment, prone to significant corrections.
